Microbiology Protocols & Mathematical Formulations

Colony counting in ColonyVision Lab adheres to heterotrophic microbiological standards from the Standard Methods for the Examination of Water and Wastewater (Section 9215).

Shape Metric
Circularity = 4π × Area Perimeter2
Also known as the Isoperimetric Quotient. Measures how closely a detected contour approximates a true geometric circle. A perfect circular colony scores 1.00, while smudges, plate scratches, or overlapping clusters score below 0.40.
Dilution Formula
CFU / mL = Colony Count (CFU) Plated Volume (mL) × 1 Dilution Factor
Calculates viable colony forming units per milliliter of the stock culture based on plated aliquot volume (e.g. 0.1 mL) and serial dilution exponent (e.g. 10-4).
Localization
(Xc, Yc) = ( m10 m00 , m01 m00 )
Computes the exact sub-pixel center of mass for each colony using OpenCV contour spatial moments mpq.
