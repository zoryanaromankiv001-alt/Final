Microbiology Protocols & Mathematical Formulations

  • Standard Methods for the Examination of Water and Wastewater (Section 9215)

    Heterotrophic Plate Count (HPC) guidelines, serial dilution factors, and viable colony counting standards.

  • Circularity Metric (Isoperimetric Quotient)

    \( \text{Circularity} = \frac{4\pi \times \text{Area}}{\text{Perimeter}^2} \). Measures how closely a detected contour approximates a true geometric circle.

  • Colony Concentration Equation

    \( \text{CFU/mL} = \frac{\text{Colony Count}}{\text{Plated Volume (mL)}} \times \frac{1}{\text{Dilution Factor}} \).
